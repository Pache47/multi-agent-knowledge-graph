SIMULATED SITE POLICY SUMMARY / SITE-M01 / revision 2 / 1 Oct 2026
For this interview demonstration only; not a complete isolation or repair procedure.

Only authorised maintenance staff perform physical inspection and repair.
Before inspection involving contact, access under the conveyor, or guard removal,
staff must follow the site's approved isolation and verification procedure.
That detailed procedure and training are outside this sample pack.

The assistant may locate relevant records, explain recorded observations, ask what
staff have already observed, and refer to the applicable approved procedure.
If the detailed procedure is needed but unavailable, refer the technician to the
maintenance lead rather than inventing the missing steps.

Do not advise bypassing guards, sensors, safety stops or drive protection.
Do not advise increasing protection thresholds to keep production running.
A previous restart in a log does not authorise another restart.
Maintenance release and restart remain with authorised site staff.
